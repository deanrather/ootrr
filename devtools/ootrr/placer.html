The Sort menu orders the enemies: HP lowest first, name A to Z, size smallest first (no size data last), habitat, or the ones you used last first. Pressing a button drops a marker only; Spawn all brings the enemies. Fliers go 2 m above Link (not when placed on a wall: the marker is where the crosshair hit). Only Remove nearest deletes a marker. Aim mode: tapping an enemy only selects it; Place at aim (or tap B, the game's put away, with the crosshair on) drops its marker where the crosshair hits, facing you (out of the wall on a wall); Remove aimed at (or hold B 0.6 s) takes away the marker nearest the crosshair and its enemy.
